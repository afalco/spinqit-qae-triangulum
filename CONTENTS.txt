Snapshot accompanying:
  F. Chinesta, A. Falco, D. Falco-Pomares,
  "On the encoding complexity of quantum numerical integration:
   an angle-structure characterization",
  arXiv:2604.24289, https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24289
  submitted to SIAM Journal on Scientific Computing (2026),
  Special Section on Quantum Computing: Numerical Algorithms and Applications.

This snapshot is identified by the git tag 'sisc-submission' at
https://github.com/afalco/spinqit-qae-triangulum
Later commits may contain improvements not reflected in the paper.

Top-level guide
  scripts/    numbered pipeline: 00 affinity check, 01 simulator, 02+04
              Triangulum runs, 03 summarisation, 05-06 QASM/job utilities,
              08 IBM Kingston submission (g0/g1/g2), 09 IBM analysis.
  src/        integrand layer, encoding operators, backends.
  data/raw/       raw simulator and hardware outputs (CSV/JSON).
  data/processed/ derived summaries used for the tables in the paper.
  data/ibm_kingston/  per-integrand IBM job results.
  docs/       experiment notes, NMR execution notes, integrand how-to.
